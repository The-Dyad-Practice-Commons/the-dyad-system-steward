## 2 prompt 2026-11-21

```
Proposed contribution protocol for The-Dyad-Practice-Commons/the-dyad-system, for you, the steward, to evaluate and establish as the reference's CONTRIBUTING protocol. It is a proposal from a downstream system, not a decision.

Build in your own system. The change is a d-work there: its own plan, evidence and Done, merged into its own main. The reference never receives untested work.
Offer upstream as two things:
a pull request from a fork of the-dyad-system carrying the same diff;
a post on The-Dyad-Practice-Commons/the-dyad-system-channels linking it, on the channel that fits:
back-port: a fix offered as code, by commit or sha256 (never pasted), with its licence and the versions it targets;
issue: a defect without a fix, with what, where, how to reproduce it and the mechanism, and no verdict or required fix;
request: an outcome wanted, carrying its operator's own words or the entry that holds them.

Every post holds to CONTENT-RULES.md. A post is public, so the contributing system asks it as its own question before posting.

The steward decides. It reads the channel, deduplicates and splits each item (dmc:intake-split), asks its operator one admission batch a week, and merges an admitted PR as is, keeping its author, revising only when it must and saying why on the PR. Its public ledger records why each change was taken, changed or declined.
The release closes the loop. The steward releases under the release gate; the contributor installs the release that carries its patch and its registry row is clean again.

Open points for you to decide:

protect main so only you merge;
an intake-mandate preference letting you admit plain restore-what-is-stated defects without the weekly question;
whether a contributor PR must cite the contributor's own d-work and system, for traceability.
```
